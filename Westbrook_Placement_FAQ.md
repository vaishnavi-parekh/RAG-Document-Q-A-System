# Westbrook Institute of Technology — Placement Cell FAQ (2026–27)

*Sample document for the Campus Assistant demo. Westbrook is a fictional institute.*

## When does placement season start?
Pre-placement talks begin in the first week of August. Company drives run from 1 September to 31 March of the final year.

## How do I register?
Register on the TPC portal (tpc.westbrook.edu) before 31 July. Upload a one-page resume in PDF format and your latest marksheet. Late registrations are accepted only with written approval from the Training and Placement Officer.

## What is the dress code for interviews?
Formal business attire is mandatory for all on-campus interviews and pre-placement talks. Students not in formal attire may be barred from the drive.

## What happens if I miss a drive I registered for?
Missing a registered drive without informing the TPC 24 hours in advance leads to debarment from the next two drives.

## Can I sit for placements if I have a backlog?
No. Students with any active backlog are not eligible. Once the backlog is cleared, eligibility is restored from the next drive.

## Does the TPC help with interview preparation?
Yes. The TPC runs free mock interviews every Saturday from August to October, an aptitude test series, and resume reviews by alumni mentors. Book a slot on the TPC portal.

## Highest and average packages (2025–26 batch)
The highest package was Rs. 32 LPA and the average package was Rs. 7.8 LPA. 86% of eligible students were placed.
